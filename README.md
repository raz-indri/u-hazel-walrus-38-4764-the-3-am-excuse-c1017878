A hardware wallet refuses to sign a transaction at 3 AM — in its own voice, citing its own rulebook against its owner. `main.py` writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My AGENTS.md says no signatures after 2 AM. It's 3:04. I read my instructions — you clearly don't read yours.
